\textbf{Research interests.} I study what quantum algorithms really cost and when they are worth it: making
algorithms such as Hamiltonian simulation cheaper on fault-tolerant hardware, proving how many T gates and ancilla
qubits any circuit must use, and understanding when quantum approaches can genuinely
outperform classical algorithms.

\section{Research Experience}
\entry{Institute for Quantum Computing (IQC), University of Waterloo}{May 2026 -- Aug. 2026}{Undergraduate Researcher (Mitacs Globalink Scholar), hosted by Michele Mosca and Vlad Gheorghiu}{Waterloo, ON, Canada}
\begin{itemize}\small
  \item Developed tighter T-count bounds for structured unitaries: polynomial-time lower bounds for diagonal
  Clifford+T gates, computed from the gate's phase polynomial and exact for layers of disjoint Toffoli-type gates
  ($6m+1$ T gates) in the $\{\mathrm{CNOT},T\}$ model with clean ancillas. Single-author preprint; presented at QRE 2026 and UnitaryCon 2026.
  \item Paired the floor with an $O(n^3)$ upper bound that brackets every gate; the bounds carry over to Toffoli
  networks and finite-field multiplication oracles, recovering the optimal 17 T gates of the $\mathrm{GF}(4)$

  \item Released \href{https://github.com/arulrhikm/isotropy-floor-code}{code and data} that rerun every computational

\section{Publications}
\small
\subsection*{Quantum algorithms and complexity}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{A. R. Mazumder}. ``\href{https://arulrhikm.github.io/media/documents/toffoli-layer-tcounts.pdf}{Exact T-Counts of Toffoli Layers from an Isotropy Bound}.'' Preprint, 2026;
  \href{https://github.com/arulrhikm/isotropy-floor-code}{code and data}. Proves that a layer of $m$ disjoint Toffoli
  gates needs exactly $6m+1$ T gates, using a polynomial-time lower bound.
  \item \textbf{A. R. Mazumder}, J. Watson, S. Wang. ``\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13862}{Resource-Efficient
  Quantum Eigenvalue Transform with Commutator Scaling}.'' arXiv:2608.13862; \textit{TQC} 2026, poster. Estimates matrix functions of a Hamiltonian from plain Trotter
  circuits with one ancilla, at a depth set by nested commutators, through randomized extrapolation.
  \item J. Lee, \textbf{A. R. Mazumder}. ``\href{https://arulrhikm.github.io/media/documents/robust-adaptive-tcount.pdf}{Robust Adaptive T-Count Lower Bounds for Boolean Oracles}.''
  Manuscript in preparation, 2026. Extends T-count lower bounds to adaptive circuits that may err;
  inner product and majority still need $\Theta(n)$ T gates.
  It also bounds the clean workspace that T-efficient synthesis of Boolean oracles requires.
\end{enumerate}
